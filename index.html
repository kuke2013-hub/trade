Important limitations

This is a browser-run experiment. Trades are simulated from available Binance spot data, not an exchange paper account. Candlestick extrema do not reveal intraminute execution order, and simulated spread/slippage may differ materially from real fills. Missing data pauses decisions; the application never invents candles or results. The +2% target and −1.5% stop are parameters, not tested optimums. No live execution, leverage, futures or shorts are supported.

Automatic scanning is attempted periodically while this tab is open; background-tab throttling, disconnected internet, CORS limits, geographic restrictions and browser shutdown can interrupt monitoring. A persistent 24/7 service and independent out-of-sample backtests are required before considering a strategy validated.
